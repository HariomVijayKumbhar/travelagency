About Rishikesh

Rishikesh is a city in India's northern state of Uttarakhand, in the foothills of the Himalayas. It's known as the 'Yoga Capital of the World' and is a spiritual destination for Hindus.

Rishikesh offers a mix of spirituality and adventure. Visit ancient temples, take yoga classes, or enjoy white-water rafting on the Ganges River. The city is also famous for its ashrams and meditation centers.

  • Yoga and Meditation: World-renowned centers
  • White-water Rafting: Thrilling Ganges adventure
  • Temples and Ashrams: Spiritual experiences
  • Trekking: Himalayan trails
  • Bungee Jumping: Adventure activities
  • Ganges Aarti: Evening prayer ceremonies
