About Mumbai

Mumbai, formerly Bombay, is the capital city of Maharashtra state and the financial, commercial, and entertainment capital of India.

Mumbai is known for its mix of modern skyscrapers & colonial buildings, vibrant nightlife, beautiful beaches, and bustling markets. Visit iconic sites like Gateway of India, Elephanta Caves, and Marine Drive while savoring diverse cuisines and cultural experiences.

  • Gateway of India: Historic monument
  • Marine Drive: Famous coastal promenade
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus: UNESCO World Heritage site
  • Elephanta Caves: Ancient rock-cut temples
  • Colaba Causeway: Bustling shopping street
  • Siddhivinayak Temple: Popular Hindu temple
